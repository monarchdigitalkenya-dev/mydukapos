# Zip 188 — what is new and how to switch it on

1. Dashboards: nothing to configure. Open the Dashboard tab in Shop, Pharmacy, Production, Services, Water, Hotel, Hospital.
2. Owner database: `node tools\setup-owner-db.mjs`, deploy, then in the owner console session call `GET /api/owner-db` (status) and `POST /api/owner-db {"action":"rebuild"}`. See docs/OWNER_DATABASE_187.md.
3. Monthly audit archive (optional, needs the audit DB from zip 187):
   - `npx wrangler r2 bucket create mydukapos-audit-archive`
   - in `wildcard-router\wrangler.toml` remove the leading `# ` from the 3 `AUDIT_ARCHIVE` lines, then `cd wildcard-router && npx wrangler deploy`
   - the router cron then archives to `audit-archive/YYYY-MM/` and prunes once a month (a backlog is worked off over a few 6-hourly firings).
   - By hand from your PC: `tools\audit-monthly.cmd` (dry run), `tools\audit-monthly.cmd run` (archive to `backup\audit-monthly\<month>`, verify, delete).
