# Beginner guide - zip 345 (plain words)

## Hospital
**Sales counter.** Search is big, items are big tiles, and each item in the cart is its own card (name on top, then quantity, price, total). Tap the amount due to change it.
**Registering a patient.**
1. Type in the **County** box (or tap the arrow). Nairobi is first. Pick one.
2. **Sub-county** then shows only that county's places. **Ward / region** shows the wards of that sub-county. You can type, search or pick in the same box.
3. Press **Register**. The patient appears in the **Just registered** strip. Tap **Send to Triage** (or the next station). No searching.
**Fewer repeated buttons.** When the patient is called, one button says "Patient is here - start consultation". The extra "Start" is gone.
**Edit** now scrolls to the form at the top, fills it, and looks the same as Add.

## Shop
**Selling something with an IMEI / serial number.** Add it to the cart. In the cart line, choose the exact IMEI or serial from the drop-down. Only units still in stock are listed.
**Items with an IMEI / serial number (phones, laptops).**
- *Adding:* tick "This item has a unique identifier per item". A box appears: type or scan one IMEI/serial per line. The number of lines becomes the quantity. Save.
- *Restocking:* in Restock add the item and the quantity, press Complete. A screen asks you to type the serial of every unit (the count must match the quantity).
- *Edit, quantity up:* change the Qty in Inventory (or Edit). A screen asks for the new serials.
- *Edit, quantity down:* a screen shows the serials in stock. Tick the one(s) that left, say why, and a manager confirms. That unit is marked written off, so it can never be sold.
**Edit profit (Profits & Expenses).**
- *Edit profit (set to 0)* - top button: every sale in the dates you are viewing gets profit 0.
- *Edit* beside one item: only that item.
- To undo: press it again and choose **Put the original profit back**.
- A manager password/PIN is asked. Your sales and money taken do not change - only the cost counted, so profit becomes 0. Every change is recorded.

## Pharmacy and Hospital stock
- **Restock**: type the **Batch ID** (as printed on the pack) and expiry on each row.
- **Put stock into a batch**: Inventory > an item > Batches. Choose the stock you already have, say how many, and either pick an existing batch or type a new Batch ID. Total stock stays the same.
- **Edit ID**: fixes a mistyped batch ID or expiry.
- **Remove from stock (recall)**: for a batch the company says is not up to standard. Choose the reason, write the notice number, enter how many, manager confirms. The units leave stock and are recorded. To only stop selling while waiting for news, use **Quarantine** instead.

## Looks
- **Light / Dark** is now remembered even on phones with full storage.
- **Receipt preview** is always white paper with black writing.
- **Notes boxes** are taller and can be pulled bigger.

## Car wash: carpets, motorbikes and more
1. Open Services, tap **+ New Task**.
2. Under **What is being cleaned** pick Car, Motorbike, Carpet, Sofa, Curtains... Cars and bikes ask for the plate; carpets ask for size.
3. Pick the services (Carpet Cleaning, Motorbike Wash...). Edit the price for big items.
4. Under **Assign to staff** tap **+ Add staff**, choose the person and what they do (Washing, Drying...).
5. Tap **Create Task**. To change the person later, open the task and use Assign.
