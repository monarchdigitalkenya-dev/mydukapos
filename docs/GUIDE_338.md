# Beginner guide - what is new in zip 338

## 1. Moving stock from the Store back to Inventory (sizes / variants)
- Store tab > press **↔ Move** on a row > type how many > **Move**.
- The stock is added to the SAME item (and the same size) in Inventory. It happens once.
- If the app is not sure which size you mean, it keeps the transfer safe and asks: Store tab > **🩺 Store check** > **Pick the items** > choose the size > **Add it to this variant**.
- Old extra items (like "Grey fleece (30)" sitting next to "Grey fleece"): Store check > "Extra items that belong to a size" > **Merge into 30**.

## 2. The Store tab
- It looks like Inventory. Click a name or code to edit it; Price and Qty are typed in the row and saved when you leave the box.
- **✏️ Edit** opens everything (cost, wholesale, reorder level, supplier, notes, sizes). **Remove** is only for correcting a mistake.
- Add new items with **➕ Add Item**, **⬆️ Add via CSV**, **📥 Import from Inventory** or **📸 Capture List**.
- **Store Stock Valuation** (top): what the store stock cost, is worth at retail and at wholesale, and the possible profit. "Full valuation report" shows it by category, top 10 and low stock; you can export CSV or print.

## 3. Categories (Settings > Category Images & Order)
- **Add:** type a name > Add category.
- **Rename:** Rename > type the new name > Save. Every item in the category changes with it. If the name exists, you are asked to merge.
- **Delete:** Delete > confirm. Items are NOT deleted; they become "Uncategorised".

## 4. Order from home (Hotel)
1. Settings > QR ordering > **Make the public QR** (one code for the whole business).
2. **Print it on every receipt** - every receipt now shows "Scan to order from home". You can also print the card or copy the link and share it on WhatsApp.
3. A customer scans it, picks food, enters name, phone, and delivery address (or "I will collect it"), and sends.
4. You hear a beep; the order is in **🍽️ Service Orders** with their phone and address. Press **Claim** to send it to the kitchen. Each customer gets their own bill called "Online: <name>".
5. To stop taking home orders: **Switch off** (it also removes the QR from the receipts).

## Hospital (HMIS) new look
1. Open the hospital app and go to **Cashier / Counter**. Add items; above the payment buttons you now see **Total due, Paid, Balance, Change** updating as you type the amount.
2. Press **COMPLETE SALE** (the big button). Hold Sale and the other buttons are quieter on purpose.
3. Go to **Reception**: the form has sections (Patient identity, Contact, Next of kin, Medical). Open **More details** for the optional fields.

## Hotel on a phone (zip 339)
Open the hotel app on your phone: the top bar is shorter and every form (Bar, Rooms, Facilities, requests) is one column with big boxes. Nothing was removed.
