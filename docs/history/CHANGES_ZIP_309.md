## Zip 309 - Multi-branch Phase 1: Business HQ (branches, staff across branches, HQ administrators, audit)

- **Business HQ** in every app (Staff tab "🏢 Business HQ" for the Admin, and the branch switcher menu): Branches, Staff, Administrators, Audit trail. Server: `functions/api/org.js` + `_org.js`; new tables in the transaction database (`migrations/0015_org.sql`, also created on first use): `org_branch`, `org_person`, `org_assignment`, `org_admin`, `org_audit` (append-only).
- **Branches:** HQ profile with a unique code, manager, contacts, location; guided Add a branch (no duplicate names / codes; plan limits still apply); Suspend (reason required, tills show "suspended by the business owner", nothing deleted) / Activate; the main branch cannot be suspended. The switcher shows "Name · CODE" and hides suspended branches.
- **Staff across branches:** one person (same record id and PIN) at one or more branches; Assign / move as *also works there*, *move permanently* or *temporary posting* (ends by itself); End at a branch; Deactivate everywhere (ends sessions and HQ access; history kept); employee numbers unique. PIN hashes never leave the server.
- **Security:** every HQ action authorised on the server on every request, deny by default; owner = main branch's management password; HQ administrators = explicit permissions over all or listed branches, sign in with their own PIN, cannot elevate themselves, manage admins or change themselves; changes and removals apply to the next request; a changed password / PIN ends the session.
- **Fixed:** the Staff tab "Move to branch" could be undone by sync (the person was cut out of the old list instead of marked removed); it is now a real move with history.
- Report: `docs/MULTIBRANCH_309.md`; audit and plan: `docs/MULTIBRANCH_AUDIT.md`. Tests: `zip309-business-hq` (7), `zip309-business-hq.browser` (Shop desktop, Hotel phone). Regression: 50 related files (branches, staff, sessions, licences, sync, payments, ledger, isolation, lint): all pass when run on their own. Service worker zip-309.
