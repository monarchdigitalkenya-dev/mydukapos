## Zip 311 - Business HQ made simple: stock moved right from the item, no approval step, fixed dialogs, steady HQ button

- **The Business HQ button no longer disappears.** It was shown only while "the Admin is serving" (re-checked every 2.5 s), so it vanished whenever a staff member was checked in on the device, and when the Staff tab redrew itself. Now it is always in the Staff tab and is put back the moment the tab redraws (HQ still asks for the owner's password or an administrator's PIN).
- **Simpler HQ:** tabs Overview, Stock, Transfers, Staff, Branches, Reports, More (Roles, HQ administrators, Audit trail, Transfer approvals).
- **Stock = one list per branch.** Every item shows its quantity on the shop floor and in each store, with **Move**, **Send to branch** and **+ Store** right on the item. A "Main store" is there for each branch without setting it up. Search, and a "Running low" filter. Move dialog: from / to with a swap button, quantity with − / +, the amount available shown, more than there is refused.
- **No approval step by default:** Send to branch takes the stock out at once (it shows as on the way); the other branch presses **Confirm received** (all good by default; "Something damaged or missing?" opens the extra boxes). Approvals can be switched on by the owner in More > Transfer approvals.
- **Every dialog** keeps its title and ✕ at the top and its buttons pinned at the bottom (no scrolling to close); on phones dialogs open as a bottom sheet. HQ always shows above the app's start screen.
- New look: ink header with underline tabs, quiet background, green for actions, amber for low stock; quantities in even-width figures.
- Server: `inv.items` (one list per branch), `xfer.create {send: true}` (create and send in one step), approvals off by default. Tests: `zip310-stock-transfers` (+1: simple mode), `zip310-hq-stock.browser` rewritten for the new screens (also checks the ✕ is visible, the Move button is on screen without scrolling, and the Staff-tab button survives a check-in and a redraw), `zip309-business-hq.browser` (audit under More).
