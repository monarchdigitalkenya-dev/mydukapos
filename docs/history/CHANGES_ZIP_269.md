## Zip 269 - Hotel: Kitchen and Bar order tabs, dish sizes, Purchases, item editing; Hospital: Kenyan lab test list; Chama removed (includes everything from zip 268)
- **Kitchen Orders and Bar Orders are two separate tabs (Hotel).** Every posted order is split by station: the kitchen's items appear on Kitchen Orders, the bar's on Bar Orders (an order with both appears on both, each with only its own items). Three columns (New / Preparing / Ready), big Start / Ready / Served buttons, the table or order, who took it, when it arrived and how long it has waited, size, extras and notes; late orders turn red and pulse, a rush order goes first (⚡). A red number on each tab counts the orders waiting, a two-tone beep sounds for each new one while the tab is open, **Full screen** turns it into a wall display and "This device is the Kitchen / Bar screen" opens it by itself (or `#kds=bar`). Statuses are the same per-item statuses the waiter's Orders tab shows (it now shows "N new · N preparing · N ready" per order and still tells the waiter when an item is ready). Replaces the single "Kitchen & Bar Display" tab (staff who had it keep both new tabs); the old "🍳 Kitchen" production tab is now **🥘 Food Tracking**. Roles: Chef -> Kitchen Orders, Bartender -> Bar Orders, Supervisor -> both. More drink words route to the bar by default (soft, coke, fanta, sprite, pepsi, tonic, mixer ...).
- **Dish sizes (Hotel).** A dish can have sizes (Small / Regular / Large, Half / Full ...) with their own price, optional cost and a recipe share (a Large uses 1.5 x the ingredients). Set them in the dish form (S / R / L and Half / Full presets). The size is chosen on the Orders tab and on the Sales Counter; the line carries it, the ingredients are taken, voided and given back with that size's share, and the kitchen screen shows it ("Beef Burger - Large"). A dish without sizes works as before. Editing a dish keeps what the form does not show (a sale price offer, its other fields).
- **Purchases screen (every module that has a Restock tab).** Each restock is now ONE purchase record (supplier, reference, date, who, its lines and total), listed for 7 / 30 / 90 days or a year, filterable by supplier or text, expandable to its lines, downloadable as CSV, with "Record a purchase" going to Restock. Stock and cost are updated by the existing restock (weighted-average cost, expense, stock movements); purchases from before this release are grouped by minute + reference + supplier.
- **Editing an existing item (Hotel).** The edit window can start (or stop) selling an item by the piece without removing and re-adding it; saving an item or dish again from the form keeps its purchase history, cost method, offers and bar settings.
- **Hospital: Kenyan lab test list.** The lab catalogue now holds about 250 tests (KMLTTB laboratory classification schedule plus the usual county, referral and private laboratory menus: haematology, chemistry, hormones, tumour markers, immunology, serology, malaria, microbiology, parasitology, cytology & histology, molecular, blood bank, toxicology). The "Lab Tests" box and the Laboratory tab's Test box are one type-or-pick field (a drop-down of every active test with its category); numeric tests are flagged against the range (set your laboratory's own in Lab catalogue).
- **Chama removed completely** (app, API, tests, routes, licence product and Owner Console entries). The applied migration `0012_chama.sql` is kept as history (empty tables are harmless). Existing Chama links no longer open an app.
- **Offline.** Verified in a real browser with the real service worker for the shop, hotel, hospital, pharmacy, production, services and water apps: open with no internet, sign in (password and staff PIN), make a cash sale (test `zip269-offline.browser.test.mjs`). A device opened for the first time on a link that never answers (Wi-Fi with no internet) now gives up after 12 s instead of waiting for ever.
- **Typing.** The patient search box in the Hospital (reception, lab, radiology, triage, queue, maternity, appointments) was rebuilt every time its tab refreshed, throwing away what was typed; it is now left alone while it holds text or the cursor.
- Tests: `zip269-dish-sizes`, `zip269-kitchen-bar-screens`, `zip269-purchases-screen`, `zip269-edit-items`, `zip269-lab-catalogue`, `zip269-offline.browser`.
