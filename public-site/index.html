Offline, explained honestly

What keeps working when the internet drops.

MyDuka saves every sale on the device first and sends it to the server when it can. Here is exactly what that means.

Works without internet

  • Selling, with cash or M-Pesa payments recorded by hand
  • Printing receipts on a printer connected to that device
  • Stock going down with each sale, and restocking
  • Reports from the records on that device
  • Signing in on a device that has been signed in before

Needs internet

  • Seeing sales made on your other devices and branches
  • Automatic M-Pesa prompts and their confirmations
  • SMS and WhatsApp messages sent by the system
  • KRA eTIMS, where it is set up
  • Opening MyDuka for the first time on a new device

When you are back online

  • Waiting sales and changes are sent automatically
  • Each sale has its own reference, so a sale sent twice is counted once
  • The Dashboard shows how many changes are still waiting

Good to know: until they are sent, waiting sales live in that device's browser storage. Do not clear the browser's data or uninstall the app while sales are waiting.
