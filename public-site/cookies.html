Cookies and local storage

Version 2026-10-01

Plain-language document. This text describes how mydukapos works and what we ask of you. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer; if your business needs a reviewed contract, contact us before relying on it.

What we use

  • Sign-in token kept in your browser so you stay signed in to the dashboard.
  • Local storage in the till apps, which holds your shop data on your device so it works offline.
  • Service worker cache so the apps open without internet.

What we do not use

The public pages set no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Page-view counts are first-party and carry no personal identifiers.

Your control

You can clear site data in your browser at any time. Clearing the till app's data removes anything not yet synced, so sync first.
