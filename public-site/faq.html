Help

Frequently asked questions

Short, honest answers. Anything missing? Ask us.

Does mydukapos work without internet?

Yes. Tills sign in and record sales on the device itself, so selling continues offline. Changes sync to your other devices when the connection returns. The website dashboard and sign-up do need internet.

Which kinds of business does it support?

Shops and supermarkets, hotels, restaurants and bars, hospitals, pharmacies, production businesses, repair/salon/service businesses and water refill stations. See the product pages on the home page.

What does it cost?

The current price and any running offer are shown on the home page and the offers page. We do not hide prices in this FAQ so they cannot go out of date.

Which devices can I use?

Any modern browser, a Windows app and an Android app.

How do I sign in?

Use the dashboard with your email and password. The same password signs in your till. If you forget it, use "Forgot password" on the dashboard.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, traffic is encrypted, and logins are rate-limited. See the Privacy Policy. You should still keep your own exports of important records.

How do I get help?

Use the contact form or WhatsApp.
