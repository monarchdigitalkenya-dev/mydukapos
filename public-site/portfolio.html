About me

I build software and websites around how a business really works.

I’m a full-stack software engineer working from Kenya under Monarch Digital Solutions. I build websites that make a business look credible, and systems that take the paperwork, guesswork and delays out of daily operations.

My work usually starts with a question, not a technology: what is slowing this business down? The answer might be a clear website, a point of sale that keeps selling when the internet drops, or a small integration that stops someone copying figures by hand.

I’m comfortable across the whole job, from the screen your customers see to the database behind it, and I stay with a project through launch, fixes and improvements.

A real example: I built and run MyDukaPOS, a point-of-sale platform with seven editions for shops, hotels and restaurants, hospitals, pharmacies, production businesses, service businesses and water stations.

  • Frontend interface design and development
  • Backend application logic and APIs
  • Database design and management
  • Business system development and customization
  • Third-party service integrations
  • Deployment, troubleshooting, maintenance and optimization
